name: Dealer 1st brand boundary
description: Durable branding and information-architecture direction for the dealership operations marketing site.
---

Dealer 1st is the product identity for this site: a BDC and finance processing partner that also develops software tools for independent dealerships. The experience should remain a first-party Dealer 1st marketing site, even when existing automotive imagery or an external site was used as visual reference.

**Why:** The initial work began as a reference recreation, but the intended deliverable is Dealer 1st's own site and should not continue to expose source-company branding or copy.

**How to apply:** Keep the dedicated Home, About Us, Services, Process, Testimonials, and Contact routes; use Dealer 1st messaging and local project assets when extending the site.
